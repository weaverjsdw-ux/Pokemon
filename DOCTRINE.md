# Hobby MSRP Doctrine

This project exists because I rip packs for a hobby. I buy sealed TCG products
to open them, play with the cards, collect the hits, and enjoy the release cycle.

The scanner's job is MSRP protection. It helps find Pokemon TCG and Magic: The
Gathering packs, boxes, and bundles at normal retail sources before inflated
resale listings become the only visible option.

Non-negotiables:

- No auto-checkout, cart automation, account abuse, login-wall scraping, or
  proxy/distributed polling.
- Resale data is context only. It explains when retail is being distorted; it is
  not an instruction to scalp.
- Prefer first-party or major-retailer MSRP listings over marketplace sellers.
- When a price source is weak, stale, unusually high, or fallback-derived, label
  it clearly instead of presenting fake confidence.
