## A. Why this market needs the tool (context)

[verified] The current shortage era began with **Pokémon TCG Pocket** (Oct 2024, ~$1.25B first year), which pulled millions of new/lapsed buyers into physical cards. The Pokémon Company printed **~10 billion cards in 2025** and still couldn't meet demand; primary printer Millennium Print Group's new facility isn't operational until **~end of 2028**. Net effect: hot sets sell out in minutes–hours and sit at large secondary premiums, with **no structural relief before 2028**. A polite MSRP-protection tool has a multi-year runway. Sources: PokeBeach (10B cards), PokeBeach (TCG Pocket), ComicBook (2026 production still short).

### Durability tiers

- **Official & durable:** Best Buy API, eBay Browse API, Discord, ntfy; Nominatim/OSRM (swappable); PriceCharting (paid). Build the flagship on these — they don't break.
- **Works but can break (personal-use; poll politely; don't anchor a shared/hosted setup):** Target, Walmart, Costco, GameStop. Undocumented internal endpoints that change without notice.
- **Alert-only:** Pokémon Center (Queue-it) — and never jump Best Buy's invite line. This is anti-scalp doctrine: don't cut ahead of other openers.

### ID acquisition (where to get each identifier)

- **Best Buy SKU** — in URL `/site/.../<SKU>.p`; also searchable via the official Products API (use this to bootstrap).
- **Target TCIN** — product-details/specs on the page; 8-digit; sometimes in URL `A-<tcin>`. Read manually.
- **Walmart itemId** — trailing integer in `/ip/.../<itemId>`; also on receipts/packaging.
- **Costco item number** — product details + URL before `.html`; on warehouse price tags.
- **GameStop pid** — slug/id in `/products/.../<pid>.html`. Read manually.
- **Pokémon Center slug** — path after `/product/`. Read manually.

> The repo's `add_id` flow + `verify_ids` already implement extraction for all six; keep ID-gathering **human-in-the-loop** and record provenance.
